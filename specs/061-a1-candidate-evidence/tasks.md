The downstream verification and acceptance requirements govern the handoff to [062](../062-a1-deployment-verification/spec.md) and [063](../063-a1-employer-acceptance/spec.md). Keep access scoped to authorized participants and exclude credentials and session tokens.

**Task markers** A task marked `[P]` can run alongside the other tasks in its group once their listed inputs are available. The checked tooling task is backed by the existing [local inventory](archive/tools.md).
