# Summary: `گزارش بچینگ (16).xlsx`

## Overview
Comprehensive **Batching Plant Production & Material Inventory Report (`گزارش بچینگ`)** for the **2,000-Unit National Housing Project in Komeil Abad, Shiraz**, tracking raw material inflows/outflows (cement silos, sand, gravel, water), ready-mix concrete output by strength grade, and precast spacer/curb production across `1403`–`1405`.

- **Source File**: `گزارش بچینگ (16).xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~3.11 MB (3,257,721 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 5
- **Date Coverage**: `1403/01/19` – `1405`

## Sheet Breakdown
| Sheet Name | Description & Key Contents |
