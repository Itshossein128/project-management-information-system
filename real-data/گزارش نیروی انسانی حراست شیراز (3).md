# Summary: `گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز (3).xlsx`

## Overview
Extended edition of the **Security Gate Attendance & Personnel Timesheet Workbook (`گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز`)** for the **2,000-Unit National Housing Project in Shiraz (Pey Chin Co.)**. It includes all sheets from `گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز.xlsx` plus 9 additional daily attendance sheets extending coverage from `1405/06/21` through `1405/06/29`.

## File Metadata
- **Source File**: `گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز (3).xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~8.72 MB (8,723,028 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 102 (`timesheet`, `sum`, and 100 daily sheets from `14050323` to `14050629`)
- **Date Coverage**: `1405/03/23` – `1405/06/29`

## Sheet Breakdown
1. **`timesheet` & `sum` Sheets**:
   - Individual employee timesheet generator (`تایم شیت پرسنل`) and cumulative attendance/overtime/leave rollups across all personnel.
2. **Daily Security Gate Logs (`14050323` – `14050629`)**:
   - **Top Summary Table (`خلاصه جمع نیروهای انسانی`)**: Daily shift headcount totals across 25 categories (HQ staff, drivers/operators, security, company laborers/tradesmen, and 19 named subcontractors).
   - **Detailed Attendance Roster (~460–500 rows per day)**: Line-by-line attendance records for every employee and subcontractor worker on site, capturing:
     - Full Name (`نام و نام خانوادگی`), Position (`سمت`), Personnel Code (`کد پرسنلی`), and Site Badge/ID (`کد شناسایی`).
     - Shift 1, Shift 2, and Shift 3 Entry (`ورود`), Exit (`خروج`), and Total Presence (`مدت حضور`).
     - Authorized Overtime (`اضافه کاری با مجوز`), Leave (`مرخصی`), and Authorized Mission (`ماموریت با مجوز`) start, end, and duration.
