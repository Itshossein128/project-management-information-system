# Summary: `اجرای ابنیه شیراز (1).xlsx`

## Overview
Comprehensive daily execution report workbook for the **Civil & Structural / Architectural Unit (`واحد اجرای ابنیه`)** of the **Komeil Abad, Shiraz National Housing Project**, containing 230 worksheets covering `1404/04/17` through `1404/12/08`.

## File Metadata
- **Source File**: `اجرای ابنیه شیراز (1).xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~14.13 MB (14,128,602 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 230 (daily sheets from `14040417` to `14041208`, plus a few working copies such as `Copy of 14040716`, `تکمیل 14040729`, etc.)
- **Date Coverage**: `1404/04/17` – `1404/12/08`

## Structure of Each Daily Sheet
1. **Header Metadata**: Company (`شرکت پی چین`), Form (`گزارش روزانه واحدهای اجرا`), Project (`پروژه مسکن ملی کمیل آباد`), Discipline (`اجرای ابنیه`), Weather, Date, Day of Week, and special site events (e.g., official visits).
2. **Executed Civil/Building Activities Table**:
   - **Execution Group (`گروه اجرایی`)**: Shift (`شیفت`), contractor or company crew (`نفر شرکت`, `خنکا`, `کشاورز`, `یزدانی`, `قدسی`, `زرافشان`, `رخساری`, etc.), skilled workers (`استاد کار`), and laborers (`کارگر`).
   - **Location (`موقعیت`)**: Zone (`قطعه`, e.g., Zones 8, 10, 12), Block (`بلوک`), and Floor (`طبقه`).
   - **Activity & Progress**: Description (`شرح فعالیت` — rebar cutting/bending, formwork, slab/stair concreting, foundation backfilling, road/parking compaction, tiling, waterproofing, door frame grouting, material hoisting), unit (`واحد`), quantity (`مقدار`), and progress percentage (`درصد اجرا`).
3. **Site Machinery Table (`ماشین‌آلات`)**: Tracks rental (`استیجاری`) and company-owned (`شرکتی`) machinery (graders, rollers, 10T–100T cranes, excavators, loaders, concrete pumps, shotcrete machines, forklifts, bobcats) with active status, operating hours, and tasks performed.
4. **Obstacles & Problems (`موانع و مشکلات`)**: Detailed daily operational issues (e.g., water pump installation delays, 3D panel shotcrete issues, scaffold plank shortages, hoist/crane breakdowns) and supervisor sign-off.
