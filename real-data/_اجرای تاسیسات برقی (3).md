# Summary: `_اجرای تاسیسات برقی (3).xlsx`

## Overview
Continuation of the daily execution reports for the **Electrical Installations Unit (`واحد اجرای تاسیسات برقی`)** at the **Komeil Abad, Shiraz National Housing Project (پروژه مسکن ملی کمیل‌آباد شیراز)**, covering spring and summer of Persian year 1405 (`1405/03/08` to `1405/06/29`).

## File Metadata
- **Source File**: `_اجرای تاسیسات برقی (3).xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~5.62 MB (5,620,906 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 101 (one sheet per day, named `14050308` through `14050629`)
- **Date Coverage**: `1405/03/08` – `1405/06/29`

## Structure of Each Daily Sheet
1. **Header Metadata**: Company (`شرکت پی چین`), Project (`پروژه مسکن ملی کمیل آباد شیراز`), Discipline (`اجرای تاسیسات برقی`), Weather Condition (`وضعیت هوا`), and Date (`تاریخ`).
2. **Executed Electrical Activities Table**:
   - **Crew Info (`گروه اجرایی`)**: Shift (`شیفت`), contractor/team name (`نام`), skilled workers (`استاد کار`), and helpers (`کارگر`).
   - **Location (`موقعیت`)**: Block (`بلوک`) and Floor (`طبقه`).
   - **Output**: Activity description (`شرح فعالیت`), unit (`واحد`), and quantity (`مقدار`).
3. **Equipment & Machinery (`ماشین‌آلات`)**: Daily status and operating hours of tools/machinery assigned to the electrical unit.
4. **Obstacles & Issues (`موانع و مشکلات`)**: Log of site/material/design issues affecting electrical progress, followed by Execution Supervisor sign-off (`امضاء سرپرست اجرا`).
