# Summary: `گزارش ماشین آلات  (3).xlsx`

## Overview
Daily **Machinery and Equipment Operations Workbook (`گزارش روزانه ماشین‌آلات و تجهیزات`)** for the **2,000-Unit National Housing Project in Shahrak-e Doran / Komeil Abad, Shiraz**, covering 188 worksheets across the first six months of Persian year 1405 (`1405/01/01` through `1405/06/30`).

## File Metadata
- **Source File**: `گزارش ماشین آلات  (3).xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~10.89 MB (10,887,923 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 188 (`14050101` through `14050630`, plus 2 copy sheets and 1 template sheet `نمونه`)
- **Date Coverage**: `1405/01/01` – `1405/06/30`

## Structure of Each Daily Sheet
Each worksheet logs approximately 69 pieces of heavy machinery, vehicles, generators, and site tools with the following fields:
- **Equipment Title (`عنوان`)**: Concrete pumps (48m, 50m), truck mixers (Benz 1–3, Amico 5, Shakmoto 265/267 day & night shifts), tower cranes (`MD310`, `MC310`), mobile cranes (10T, 28T), loaders (Komatsu 470, W90), Hyundai bobcat, 3T forklift, Ferguson tractor, diesel compressors, climbers (`کلایمر 1 تا 10`), rebar cutters/benders/stirrup machines (`قیچی`, `خم‌کن`, `خاموت‌زن`), pickups & passenger vehicles (Mazda, Nissan, Samand, Peugeot 405/Pars, Tiggo 7 Pro, Hyundai Tucson, KMC K7), motorcycles, hoists (`بالابر 1 تا 4`), vibrators, 60T weighbridge (`باسکول 60 تنی`), 1.5m³ batching plant, Volvo diesel generators (`40KVA` to `400KVA`), and water tankers.
- **Operational Status (`شیفت روز`)**: Checkboxes (`True`/`False`) for Active (`فعال`), Standby/Ready (`آماده`), and Broken/Down (`خراب`).
- **Ownership (`نوع مالکیت`)**: Owned (`تملیکی`) vs. Rented (`اجاره‌ای`).
- **Time Tracking**: Start time (`ساعت شروع کار`), End time (`ساعت پایان کار`), Repair duration (`تعمیرات (ساعت)`), and Net useful operation (`کارکرد مفید`).
- **Remarks & Asset ID**: Fault/maintenance notes (`توضیحات`, e.g., flat tire, oil leak, water pump failure, alternator repair) and **Elka ERP Asset ID (`آی‌دی دستگاه در الکا`**, e.g., `10003`–`10183`).
