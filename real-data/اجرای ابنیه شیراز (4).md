# Summary: `اجرای ابنیه شیراز (4).xlsx`

## Overview
Continuation of the daily execution report workbook for the **Civil & Building Works Unit (`واحد اجرای ابنیه`)** at the **Komeil Abad, Shiraz National Housing Project**, covering 99 daily worksheets from `1404/12/08` and `1405/03/23` through `1405/06/27`.

## File Metadata
- **Source File**: `اجرای ابنیه شیراز (4).xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~6.15 MB (6,149,694 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 99 (one sheet per day: `14041208` followed by `14050323` through `14050627`)
- **Date Coverage**: `1404/12/08` and `1405/03/23` – `1405/06/27`

## Structure of Each Daily Sheet
1. **Header Metadata**: Company (`شرکت پی چین`), Form (`گزارش روزانه واحدهای اجرا`), Project (`پروژه مسکن ملی کمیل آباد`), Discipline (`اجرای ابنیه`), Weather Condition, Date, and Day of Week.
2. **Executed Civil/Architectural Activities Table**:
   - **Crew Breakdown (`گروه اجرایی`)**: Shift, contractor/company team name, number of master craftsmen (`استاد کار`) and laborers (`کارگر`).
   - **Location (`موقعیت`)**: Zone (`قطعه`), Block (`بلوک`), and Floor (`طبقه`).
   - **Work Output**: Activity description (`شرح فعالیت`), measurement unit (`واحد`), quantity (`مقدار`), and completion percentage (`درصد اجرا`).
3. **Machinery Log (`ماشین‌آلات`)**: Status, ownership (`استیجاری` / `شرکتی`), operating hours, and daily task descriptions for heavy equipment on site.
4. **Obstacles & Bottlenecks (`موانع و مشکلات`)**: Daily log of site, material, and equipment issues signed off by the Execution Supervisor (`امضاء سرپرست اجرا`).
