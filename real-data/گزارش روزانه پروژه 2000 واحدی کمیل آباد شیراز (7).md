## Structure of Each Daily Report Sheet
Follows the standard contractual daily report template:
1. **Header & Weather**: Employer, Consultant, Date, Report No., Contract No. (`1402/25319/ص`), Shift hours (Shifts 1–3), Workshop status, and Weather conditions.
2. **Manpower Breakdown**: Indirect/HQ personnel (`نیروی انسانی غیرمستقیم`) and Direct execution/operator personnel (`نیروی انسانی مستقیم`) with present and on-leave headcounts.
3. **Materials & Concrete**: Daily incoming materials (`مصالح وارده`) and concrete volume poured (`حجم بتن ریخته شده`).
4. **Machinery & Equipment**: Count and operational status of site machinery and vehicles.
5. **Executed & General Site Activities**: Detailed breakdown of construction activities by contractor, zone (`قطعه`), block (`بلوک`), floor (`طبقه`), location, and quantity.
6. **Contractual Obstacles, Visits & Sign-offs**: Delay notifications, official visitors log, and sign-offs by Project Control, Technical Office, and Site Superintendent.
