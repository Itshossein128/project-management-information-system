# Summary: `گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز.xlsx`

## Overview
Daily **Security Gate Attendance & Personnel Timesheet Workbook (`گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز`)** for the **2,000-Unit National Housing Project in Shiraz (Pey Chin Co.)**, containing individual timesheet summaries and 91 daily attendance sheets from `1405/03/23` to `1405/06/20`.

## File Metadata
- **Source File**: `گزارش نیروی انسانی حراست شیراز.xlsx`
- **File Size**: ~8.12 MB (8,118,760 bytes)
- **Total Sheets**: 93 (`timesheet`, `sum`, and 91 daily sheets from `14050323` to `14050620`)
- **Date Coverage**: `1405/03/23` – `1405/06/20` (plus monthly timesheet data from `1404`)

## Sheet Breakdown
1. **`timesheet` & `sum` Sheets**:
   - Individual monthly timesheets (`تایم شیت پرسنل کارگاه کمیل آباد`) and aggregated attendance summaries (`کل کارکرد`, `کل اضافه کاری`, `کل کسر کار`, `کل مرخصی`) by employee code and name.
2. **Daily Security Attendance Sheets (`14050323` – `14050620`)**:
   - **Summary Table (`خلاصه جمع نیروهای انسانی` — Rows 3–31)**: Aggregated headcount across Shifts 1, 2, and 3 for 25 personnel/contractor groups:
     1. HQ Staff (`نیروهای ستادی`)
     2. Drivers & Operators (`راننده و اپراتور`)
     3. Security & Camp Bosses (`حراست`)
     4. Company Daily-Wage Laborers (`کارگر روزمزد شرکتی`)
     5. Company Skilled Tradesmen (`استادکار روزمزد شرکتی`)
     6–24. Subcontractors (`پیمانکاران`: Khaneka, Etminan, Hosseini, Derakhshandeh, Rezaei Electrical, Moezi, Kharestani, Nourbakhsh, Keshtkar, Keyhani, Rezaei Screed, Bahari, Ghodsi, Farahtaj, Delhazin, Pakniyat, Yazdani, Miscellaneous) and Branch/Marvdasht personnel.
   - **Detailed Personnel Roster (Rows 33–500)**: Individual clock-in (`ورود`), clock-out (`خروج`), and attendance duration (`مدت حضور`) across Shifts 1, 2, and 3, plus authorized overtime (`اضافه کاری با مجوز`), leave (`مرخصی`), and missions (`ماموریت با مجوز`) with personnel code (`کد پرسنلی`), ID code (`کد شناسایی`), full name, and role.
