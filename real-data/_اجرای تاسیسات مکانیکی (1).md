# Summary: `_اجرای تاسیسات مکانیکی (1).xlsx`

## Overview
Daily execution report workbook for the **Mechanical Installations Unit (`واحد اجرای تاسیسات مکانیکی`)** of the **Komeil Abad, Shiraz National Housing Project (پروژه مسکن ملی کمیل‌آباد شیراز)**, containing 54 daily sheets spanning late `1404` and summer `1405`.

  - `1405/04/01` – `1405/04/19` (19 sheets)

  - `1405/06/01` – `1405/06/29` (29 sheets)

## Structure of Each Daily Sheet
1. **Header Metadata**: Form title (`گزارش روزانه واحدهای اجرا`), Project (`پروژه مسکن ملی کمیل آباد شیراز`), Discipline (`اجرای تاسیسات مکانیکی`), Weather condition, and Date.
2. **Executed Mechanical Activities Table**:
   - **Execution Group (`گروه اجرایی`)**: Shift (`شیفت`), contractor name (`نام`, e.g., `کشتکار`), and headcount (`تعداد نفرات`).
   - **Location (`موقعیت`)**: Zone (`قطعه`), Block (`بلوک`), and Floor (`طبقه`).
   - **Work Output**: Activity description (`شرح فعالیت` — e.g., water supply, drainage, gas piping, HVAC ducting), unit (`واحد`), and quantity (`مقدار`).
3. **Machinery (`ماشین‌آلات`)**: Equipment usage and active/inactive hours.
4. **Obstacles & Recommendations (`موانع و مشکلات`)**: Notes on completed blocks (e.g., completion of Blocks `32-33` and `40-41`) and material procurement requests for upcoming blocks.
