# Summary: `گزارش بالانس مصالح شیراز.xlsx`

## Overview
Material balance, estimation, inventory flow, and financial summary workbook (`گزارش بالانس مصالح`) for the **Komeil Abad, Shiraz National Housing Project**. It compares estimated quantities per block and across the entire project against material requisitions, warehouse receipts (`مقدار وارده`), warehouse issues (`مقدار خارج شده از انبار`), and consumption by zone (`قطعه`).
